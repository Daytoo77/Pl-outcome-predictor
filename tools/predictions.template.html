The model's call on every match it was never shown, and how it held up. Test-season matches were predicted by a bundle tuned on 2021-23; holdout matches by the committed model, tuned on 2023-25. Each was blind to the season it is scoring.

Probabilities are the calibrated blend of the classifier and the Dixon–Coles goal model, shown to one decimal place. "What moved the pick" lists the classifier's largest signed feature contributions toward its predicted class, in log-odds. The scoreline grid is the Dixon–Coles matrix for the model's expected goals. Model probabilities are computed from pre-kickoff information for study purposes; they are not betting advice.
